Aura NeuroTech

A new identity and interface language for a neurotech company whose big idea was hard to explain.

01 / 05
Year2026
ServicesBrand identity, Spatial UI
ClientAura NeuroTech
RoleCreative direction & design

The story

Making a complicated idea feel obvious.

The challenge

Aura NeuroTech was building something genuinely new, but the story around it was crowded with jargon. The brand needed to feel credible to scientists and approachable to everyone else.

The approach

We started with one clear idea and built the identity around it: a calm, glowing visual language, a flexible logo system, and a spatial interface style that carries into product and pitch decks.

The result

What we delivered.

A complete identity system with usage guidelines
Spatial UI concepts for product and presentations
A clearer story for investors, partners and press
Assets ready for web, print and motion
