Privacy

Privacy note

Last updated: September 2026. This is a short, plain-language note about what this website does with your information.

What I collect

Only what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email address, project details and message. If you email me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you write.

How it is used

To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project. I do not sell your details or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who handles it

  • Form messages are delivered through a form service (Formspree), or directly by email if the service is not connected.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, and some images load from Unsplash and Pexels. Those services may receive your IP address when they serve files.

Cookies and storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a single setting, your light or dark theme choice, on your own device.

Your choices

You can ask me to delete or correct anything you have sent by emailing hello@madebykirat.com.
